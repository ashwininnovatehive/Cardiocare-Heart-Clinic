EP Study and Radiofrequency Ablation

Home  /  EP Study and Radiofrequency Ablation

An Electrophysiology (EP) Study is a specialized test that evaluates your heart's electrical system to diagnose abnormal heartbeats or arrhythmias. It provides a detailed map of how electrical signals travel through the heart, helping to pinpoint the source of a rhythm problem.

Following an EP study, Radiofrequency Ablation is often performed in the same session. This is a curative procedure that uses radiofrequency energy (similar to microwave heat) to destroy the small area of heart tissue that is causing the arrhythmia.

What is an EP Study?

During an EP study, thin, flexible wires called catheters are inserted into a vein and guided to your heart. These catheters can sense electrical activity and stimulate the heart, allowing your cardiologist to discover where an arrhythmia is coming from and determine the best course of treatment.

How Does Radiofrequency Ablation Work?

  • Once the source of the arrhythmia is identified, the tip of a special catheter delivers radiofrequency energy to the precise location.
  • This energy creates a tiny scar, which blocks the abnormal electrical pathway.
  • By interrupting the faulty circuit, the procedure can cure many types of arrhythmias, often eliminating the need for long-term medication.
  • It is highly effective for conditions like Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT) and Atrial Fibrillation.
