Cardiology

Cardiology is a branch of medicine that deals with the disorders of the heart and blood vessels. It focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of heart diseases including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and congenital heart defects. Cardiologists use advanced diagnostic techniques and treatments to help patients maintain heart health.
