Pacemaker, CRT & ICDs Implantation

Home  /  Pacemaker, CRT & ICDs Implantation

Cardiac device implantation involves placing small, sophisticated electronic devices in the body to manage and treat abnormal heart rhythms and heart failure. These devices are critical for patients whose heart's natural electrical system is not functioning correctly. At Cardiocare, we provide expert implantation of various cardiac devices.

Types of Cardiac Devices

  • Pacemakers: Used to treat slow heart rhythms (bradycardia). A pacemaker sends electrical pulses to the heart to maintain a normal heart rate.
  • Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillators (ICDs): These devices are for patients at risk of life-threateningly fast heart rhythms (tachycardia). An ICD continuously monitors the heart and can deliver an electrical shock to restore a normal rhythm.
  • Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy (CRT): For patients with heart failure, CRT devices (often called biventricular pacemakers) use leads on both sides of the heart to coordinate contractions and improve its pumping efficiency.

The Implantation Procedure

Device implantation is typically a minor surgical procedure. It is performed under local anesthesia, and involves making a small incision near the collarbone to create a pocket for the device. Leads are then guided through a vein into the heart, positioned, and connected to the device.
