Cardiology

Home  /  Cardiology

Cardiology is a specialized branch of medicine focused on the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases and disorders of the heart and blood vessels — the cardiovascular system. At Cardiocare Heart Clinic, we provide comprehensive care for a wide range of heart conditions, from common issues like hypertension to complex cardiac events.

Our lead cardiologist, Dr. Shrikant Naigude, utilizes state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and evidence-based treatments to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients. We believe in a patient-centric approach, focusing on education, personalized care plans, and long-term management strategies to promote heart health.

Conditions We Treat

  • Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) - Blockages in the heart's arteries.
  • Heart Failure - The heart's inability to pump blood effectively.
  • Arrhythmias - Irregular heart rhythms.
  • Hypertension - High blood pressure.
  • Valvular Heart Disease - Issues with one or more heart valves.
  • Congenital Heart Defects - Heart abnormalities present at birth.

Our Diagnostic Approach

Accurate diagnosis is the first step toward effective treatment. We employ a variety of advanced diagnostic tests, including Electrocardiograms (ECG), Echocardiograms, Stress Tests, and Cardiac Catheterization, to get a complete picture of your heart's health.
